Household Ready (Prêt 72h) — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-11

English · Français

Local-first emergency profile

Household Ready stores your emergency contacts, meeting plan, optional medication/allergy notes, 72-hour kit inventory, document locations (not document files), cash-buffer notes, and drill reminders in encrypted local storage on your device. No account is required for the core product.

Encryption

A passphrase unlocks the on-device vault. Current builds use an MVP passphrase-derived keystream (not AES). This protects casual access on a locked phone; it is not a password manager and is not bank-grade cryptography. We disclose this honestly in-app.

Sensitive (medical) fields

Optional medications, allergies, and related notes stay on device. They are never uploaded by default. You may include or exclude them when printing a sheet or exporting a backup — that share leaves the device under your control via the OS share sheet.

Backups and print

Encrypted export and printable emergency sheets are initiated by you. We do not operate a cloud sync for your profile in the MVP.

Analytics and ads

We do not embed advertising SDKs. Crash or analytics tooling, if added later, will be limited to technical diagnostics and disclosed here.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from children.
