Business Pocket (Poche Métier Offline) — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-11

English · Français

Who this is for

Business Pocket helps independents (drivers, tradespeople, freelancers) keep day-of customers, jobs, local invoices, expenses, and emergency continuity contacts on the device.

Local business data

Rides/jobs, customers, invoice amounts, expenses, and continuity fields are stored locally. Core day workflows work offline. Optional API sync, when configured in a build, may send business records to your configured backend — disclose that host in the build notes; it is not required for offline use.

Invoices honesty

Local numbered invoices are not certified electronic invoicing (e.g. French PDP). They are a thin offline record for your own accounting workflow.

Encrypted backup

Passphrase-protected backup export/import (when unlocked via Pro features) is user-initiated. MVP cryptography is on-device; treat backups as sensitive.

Analytics and ads

No advertising SDKs in the core product. Crash tooling, if added, is for diagnostics only.
