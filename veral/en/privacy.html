DebtTrack — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-02

Your data stays on your device

DebtTrack (VERAL) keeps your personal debt and receivable records only in an on-device SQLite database. There is no in-app account and no cloud sync operated by us.

What you enter

You may enter contact names, amounts, dates, notes, payment methods, reminders, and optional photos. All of this stays on your device unless you export or share it yourself.

Analytics and crash reporting

We do not embed third-party advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs. Optional crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics) sends technical error diagnostics only — never your debt records, contacts, or amounts.

Data safety summary

Data collected: Content you enter — stored locally.
Sharing: By default nothing is sent to the developer or ad networks.
Deletion: Reset data in Settings or uninstall the app.
Children: The app does not knowingly collect data from children.
