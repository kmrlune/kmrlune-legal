BalanceLens — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-30

English · Français

Your data stays on your device by default

BalanceLens keeps income, expense, and budget records in local storage on your device. There is no required account for offline use.

Optional cloud sync

If Firebase is configured for your build, authentication and synced financial data may be stored with that service under your control. Disable or omit Firebase to keep data device-only.

Analytics and ads

We do not embed third-party advertising SDKs. Crash or analytics tooling, if present in a given build, is limited to technical diagnostics.

Data safety summary

Data collected: Categories, amounts, notes, and dates you enter — stored locally (and optionally in Firebase when enabled).

Sharing: By default nothing is sent to ad networks.

Deletion: Clear app data or uninstall to remove local records; remove Firebase account data via that service if sync was used.

Children: The app does not knowingly collect data from children.
