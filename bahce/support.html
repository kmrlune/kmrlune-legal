Bahce — Support

About the app

Bahce (Bahçe) is a local-first garden and stall vegetable sales tracker. Sales, buyers, and balances stay on your device — no account required.

How to get help

Email support: ahadogmus@icloud.com

Please include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy for how data is handled on device.

As-is

The app is provided “as is”. You are responsible for the accuracy of entries you record and for backing up device data before uninstalling.
