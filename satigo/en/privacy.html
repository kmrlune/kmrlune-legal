Satigo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-05

English · Français · Türkçe · Español · العربية

Your data stays on your device

Satigo keeps market and event sales records — products, baskets, buyers, balances, and pre-orders — only in an on-device SQLite database. There is no in-app account and no cloud sync operated by us.

What you enter

You may enter product names, quantities, prices, buyer names, payment status (paid / on tab / partial), notes, photos you attach, and related business figures. All of this stays on your device unless you export or share it yourself.

Camera and contacts

Camera access is used only to scan product barcodes when you choose to. Contact access is used only to fill buyer name, phone, or email when you pick someone; Satigo never changes contacts on your phone.

Analytics and crash reporting

We do not embed third-party advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs. Optional crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics) sends technical error diagnostics only — never your sales ledger, buyers, or product catalog.

Data safety summary

Data collected: Business content you enter (products, sales, buyers, amounts, dates, optional photos) — stored locally.

Sharing: By default nothing is sent to the developer or ad networks.

Deletion: Clear app data from device settings or uninstall the app to remove the local database.

Children: The app does not knowingly collect data from children.
