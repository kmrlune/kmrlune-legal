PhotoIdent — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-27

English · Français

On-device processing

PhotoIdent prepares and checks ID photos only on your device. Images are not sent to a server we operate; there is no in-app account or cloud sync.

What you provide

You may capture or import photos, crop them, and save them locally (“My photos” history and optionally the device gallery). Those files stay under your control unless you share or export them yourself.

Crash reporting

We do not embed advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs. Optional crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics) may send technical diagnostics — never the content of your photos.

Not a certification

PhotoIdent is a technical helper and does not certify administrative acceptance. For a French title, a photo from an authorized professional or approved booth is generally still required.

Data safety summary

Data collected: Photos you capture or import, validation metadata (scores, rules), preferences (language, theme) — stored locally.
Sharing: By default nothing is sent to the developer or ad networks.
Deletion: Clear local history in Settings → Privacy & data, or uninstall the app.
Children: The app does not knowingly collect data from children.
