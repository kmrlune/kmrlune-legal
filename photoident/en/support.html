About the app

PhotoIdent helps you prepare French-style ID photos on device: capture or import, crop to official dimensions, run compliance checks, and export print sheets. There is no account and no cloud upload.

How to get help

Email support: support@monorp.com
Please include your device model, OS version, and a short description of the issue.

Privacy

As-is

The app is provided “as is”. Automatic checks are a helper only — they do not guarantee acceptance by an administration. For French titles, an authorized capture is generally still required.
